Shop Rules

All booster packs are virtual and unofficial. The current catalog has 33 collectible cards from the Pokémon Card Arena's classic Kanto set: Pokémon, Energy, and Trainer cards. Rare and Ultra Rare shop tiers use lower selection weights. Pack contents are selected randomly by the server, not the browser.

A signed-in CTooniverse account and sufficient Cartoon Coins are required to purchase packs. Purchases are final once confirmed. The Pokémon Card Arena currently uses its own fixed loaner decks, so owning cards here doesn't change your playable deck yet.
