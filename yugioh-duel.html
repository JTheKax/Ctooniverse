    How to play & what the simplified rules include

    Both duelists start with 8,000 Life Points and 5 cards. You can Normal Summon or Set one monster per turn. Level 5–6 monsters need one tribute; Level 7 or higher need two, taken from your weakest monsters automatically. Use Spell cards and set Trap cards in the Main Phase. Traps can activate on a later turn.

    After your opening turn, choose Battle Phase, tap one of your Attack Position monsters, then tap an opponent's monster to attack it. If your opponent has none, choose Attack Directly. Each monster can attack once per turn. Attack vs Attack compares ATK and damages the loser. Attack vs Defense compares ATK and DEF; attacking a stronger defender costs Life Points. End Turn runs the computer's turn and draws your next card.

    This is a simplified fan-made prototype, not the official Yu-Gi-Oh! rules engine. You duel using the 40 Main Deck cards from your permanently selected starter. Original card artwork is served from CTooniverse Storage. Many Spell and Trap card effects are simplified prototype effects, not their official Yu-Gi-Oh! effects. The Extra Deck is displayed but Extra Deck summoning is not implemented. Starter cards are Arena-only; they are not added to your collection. Opponent-themed computer decks are available from the Free Duel roster. No online duels or duel-win rewards are included.
