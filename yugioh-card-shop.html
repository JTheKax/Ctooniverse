Shop Rules

These are unofficial virtual booster packs purchased with in-game Cartoon Coins, not physical Yu-Gi-Oh! products. Every pack awards five distinct random cards from its stated pool; repeat cards across separate openings increase your quantity. Packs do not claim official Konami card lists or official rarity odds.

Complete collection: The eight Card Archive volumes divide all 14,681 imported English-language cards into separate groups, so every catalog card can be found in at least one pack. The archives include collectible Tokens and Skill Cards; those cannot be used in the current Deck Builder or Duel Arena. The other packs focus on specific monsters, attributes, mechanics and iconic series.

Starter decks (Yugi, Kaiba, Joey, Pegasus) are locked Arena loaners. They do not grant ownership. Your custom Deck Builder remains a reference editor; its drafts do not award cards or automatically become playable Arena decks.

Card data: YGOPRODeck API v7. Artwork © Konami / 4K Media. Unofficial fan project.
