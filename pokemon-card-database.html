About This Database

The imported index covers English card printings currently listed by TCGdex, across classic and modern expansions. Each set/card ID identifies a distinct printing, including reprints. Card-specific details load from the TCGdex reference API when you open a card. Some records don't have images or full card text yet.

Booster rewards and owned collections are handled separately on CTooniverse. The eight Archive volumes together cover every indexed card. The Pokémon Card Arena currently uses its own simplified loaner decks, not your collected cards.

Data and card imagery provided by TCGdex. Pokémon trademarks and imagery belong to their respective owners. Unofficial, non-endorsed fan project.
