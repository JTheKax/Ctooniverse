Console Compatibility

GB · GBC · GBA · NES · SNES · N64 · Genesis · DS · PS1. Game compatibility varies by core, console, and device. The player runs EmulatorJS in an isolated embedded frame and loads its stable browser cores. Some games may require a BIOS you own or more memory than a phone can provide.

CTooniverse does not supply copyrighted game downloads or BIOS files. Only use or publish content you own or have permission to use. EmulatorJS supported systems and documentation.
