Deck Builder Rules

The Main Deck needs 40–60 cards to be ready. The Extra Deck and Side Deck can each have up to 15 cards. You can have up to 3 copies of each card across all three decks combined. Fusion, Synchro, Xyz and Link Monsters belong in the Extra Deck; the Side Deck can hold either type. You can save an unfinished draft.

Deck-size and copy checks are built in, but this builder does not yet certify tournament legality. Always check the latest official Forbidden & Limited List and card release eligibility. Your editable custom deck drafts do not alter your permanently chosen Arena starter or award any owned cards.
