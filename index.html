What the navigation colors mean

The colors on the website tabs and dropdown menus tell you whether CTooniverse is live or under maintenance.

GREEN — LIVE

The website is online and operating normally. You can browse and play available games.

YELLOW — MAINTENANCE

The website is being updated or repaired. Some pages, games, and features may be temporarily unavailable.

These colors apply to navigation tabs and dropdown menus on desktop and mobile. The color examples above explain the rules; they do not indicate the current site status.
