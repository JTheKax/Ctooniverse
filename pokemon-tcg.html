    How this Pokémon card game works

    Start with a 60-card deck, seven cards in hand and six Prize cards. You automatically begin with a Basic Pokémon. Play other Basic Pokémon onto your Bench (maximum five), evolve a Pokémon after it has spent at least a turn in play, and attach one Energy per turn. Your Active Pokémon attacks once enough Energy is attached. Attacking ends your turn. Each knocked-out Pokémon lets the attacking Trainer take one Prize card. The first Trainer to take all six prizes, knock out the opponent's final Pokémon, or leave an opponent unable to draw wins.

    Trainer effects, damage, weaknesses, retreat, and available attacks are simplified for this prototype. All decks and cards are loaned for play: no packs, card ownership, or coins are affected. Matches and opponents are computer-controlled only.
